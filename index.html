Turn moments into short videos — automatically

A Windows desktop app for creators. Everything runs on your own PC.

Find the best moments

Paste a link to your own stream or video, or import a recording. Monkey Crazy listens, transcribes and finds the funniest, most exciting moments.

Edit vertical shorts

Each moment becomes a 9:16 video that follows the action and faces, keeps the original sound and adds word-by-word subtitles.

Post to your channels

Review, schedule and post to your own YouTube and TikTok accounts after you connect them — nothing is posted without your approval or your auto-post setting.

Trends & analytics

See what's trending (YouTube, Twitch, Google Trends) and how your posted videos perform, all inside the app.

How Monkey Crazy uses your accounts

Monkey Crazy is a desktop application, not a web service. When you connect YouTube or TikTok, the app signs in through the platform's official sign-in page and keeps the access it receives encrypted on your computer. It uses that access only to upload the videos you approve, show your channel name, and read the statistics of the videos it posted for you. There is no Monkey Crazy server and the developer never receives your account data. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
